  https://database.lichess.org/lichess_db_puzzle.csv.zst

Local manifest: public/data/puzzle-manifest.json
Local pack SHA-256:
  94b2464985fdab237640a2e951febef51a8ded71774b8d0d538d3b6fc509dbb6

The generator read 194,275 source rows, retained ratings 800–1900 with
popularity at least 80 and at least 40 plays, removed duplicates, and emitted
3,000 puzzles. Of those, 600 were selected for Pirc/KID/KIA structure tags.
Puzzle IDs and source-game URLs are retained for traceability. The
transformation and project-authored explanations are part of King's Study;
the underlying puzzle records remain CC0.
